Privacy Policy

FocusFlow is designed to help you focus without sending your browsing data to the developer or third parties. The extension processes the minimum information needed to run its timer and website blocker.

Effective date
July 11, 2026
Applies to
FocusFlow Chrome extension

01

Overview

FocusFlow does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or external data servers. Information needed by the extension is processed locally in your browser.

02

Information processed

FocusFlow locally processes and stores:

  • Domains you add to the blocked-sites list.
  • Whether each blocked domain is enabled.
  • Timer durations, sound volume, mode, and session state.
  • Website-blocking preferences.

The hostname of a page you navigate to is checked in memory against your blocked-sites list. FocusFlow does not save a general browsing history or record the content of pages you visit.

03

Information not collected

FocusFlow does not collect or access:

  • Names, email addresses, or account credentials.
  • Passwords, cookies, payment details, or form data.
  • The text, images, messages, or other content of web pages.
  • Location, contacts, files, or device identifiers.

04

Why permissions are needed

Permission Purpose
storage Saves timer settings and blocked domains locally.
declarativeNetRequest Blocks or redirects domains selected by you.
webNavigation Detects navigation on SPA websites such as YouTube.
host access Allows blocking to work on domains you add.
alarms Completes timers while the side panel is closed.
offscreen Plays the local timer sound in the background.
favicon Displays icons for domains in your blocked list.
sidePanel Displays the FocusFlow interface in Chrome.

05

Storage and retention

Settings and blocked domains are stored using chrome.storage.local on your device. They remain there until you edit them, remove them, clear the extension's storage, or uninstall FocusFlow.

06

Sharing and transmission

FocusFlow does not transmit, sell, rent, or share your stored data with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The timer sound is bundled with the extension and is played locally.

07

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when FocusFlow's functionality or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.
